# VEGA research brief

Research checked August 12, 2026. VEGA is an independent student concept, not an official CCSD project.

## Policy requirements reflected in the prototype

- CCSD's Acceptable Use Policy limits network resources to educational or professional purposes, requires protection of personally identifiable information, calls for encryption, permits monitoring, and requires third-party sharing to be approved and FERPA-compliant.
- Its AI section calls for fair, ethical, unbiased use; critical evaluation of outputs; no sensitive information in external AI tools; and prohibits deepfakes, plagiarism, false information, and unauthorized automation.
- CCSD's student-data privacy process references FERPA, COPPA, PPRA, CIPA, HIPAA, IDEA, and Nevada requirements, with district-level technology review.
- Approved electronic communications must use a district professional account, be logged, be educational and safe, comply with privacy law, and allow voluntary participation or opt-out.

## Claims discipline

The site labels proposed controls and simulated capabilities. Air cooling is a concept requiring engineering validation. VEGA does not claim zero environmental impact, official approval, access to school records, or a deployed local model.
